Step-by-Step Guides

Download & Activate on Any Device

Choose your device below and follow the simple steps to start streaming in minutes.

Smart TV Guide

Samsung · LG · Android TV · Philips

App: Hot IPTV
1

Download Hot IPTV on your Smart TV

On your Smart TV go to the App Store (Samsung Smart Hub / LG Content Store). Search for Hot IPTV and click Install.

2

Open the App & Find your MAC Address

Launch Hot IPTV. On the home screen you will see your MAC Address displayed. Note it down — you will need it in the next step.

3

Go to the Activation Website

On your phone or computer open hotplayer.app/upload. Enter your MAC Address and the M3U URL we sent you after payment. Click Save.

Restart the App & Watch!

Go back to your Smart TV, close and reopen Hot IPTV. Your channels will load automatically. Enjoy 60,000+ channels in 4K!

Android Guide

Android Phone · Android TV Box · Tablet

App: IB Player
1

Download IB Player

Open the Google Play Store, search for IB Player and tap Install.

2

Open IB Player & Get your MAC Address and Device Key

Launch the app. On the home screen you will see your MAC Address and Device Key. Note both down.

3

Go to the Activation Website

On your browser go to iboplayer.pro/manage-playlists/login/. Enter your MAC Address and Device Key then click Login.

4

Add Playlist & Enter your URL

Click Add Playlist. Paste the M3U URL we sent you after payment into the URL field. Click Save.

Restart the App & Watch!

Go back to IB Player on your device, close and reopen it. All your channels will load. Enjoy!

MAG Device Guide

MAG 250 · MAG 254 · MAG 322 · MAG 420

Portal Activation

Important: To activate your MAG device we need your MAC Address. Once you send it to us via WhatsApp, we will give you the portal URL to enter on your device.

1

Find Your MAC Address

Power on your MAG box. Go to Settings → System Information. You will see your MAC Address — it looks like: 00:1A:79:XX:XX:XX

2

Send Us Your MAC Address

Send your MAC address to us on WhatsApp. We will register it on our server and send you back the Portal URL within minutes.

3

Enter the Portal URL

On your MAG box go to Settings → System Settings → Servers → Portals. Enter the Portal URL we sent you in the Portal 1 URL field.

4

Restart & Connect

Save the settings and restart your MAG box. It will automatically connect to the portal and load all your channels.

You're All Set!

Your MAG device is now activated and ready to stream all channels and content.

Amazon Fire Stick Guide

Fire Stick 4K · Fire Stick Lite · Fire TV Cube

App: Hot Player
1

Enable "Apps from Unknown Sources"

On your Fire Stick go to Settings → My Fire TV → Developer Options. Turn on Apps from Unknown Sources.

2

Download the "Downloader" App

Go back to the home screen. In the Search bar type Downloader (by AFTVnews). Install it — this app lets you sideload APKs on Fire Stick.

3

Open Downloader & Get Hot Player

Open Downloader. In the URL box type the Hot Player APK link we provide you on WhatsApp and tap Go. The APK will download automatically.

4

Install Hot Player

Once downloaded, tap Install on the prompt screen. After installation tap Open to launch Hot Player.

5

Find your MAC Address

On the Hot Player home screen your MAC Address is displayed. Note it down.

6

Activate at hotplayer.app/upload

On your phone or computer go to hotplayer.app/upload. Enter your MAC Address and the M3U URL we sent you after payment. Click Save.

Restart & Stream!

Close and reopen Hot Player on your Fire Stick. Your 60,000+ channels will load automatically. Enjoy!
